FAQ

Which casinos have mobile apps?

Some larger brands, such as Stake and BC.Game, offer native apps or installable web apps, while others focus solely on mobile‑optimized browser play. Always download any app through the official casino website or a trusted app store listing to reduce the risk of fake apps.

Do these casinos require KYC?

Many crypto casinos allow registration and smaller withdrawals with limited upfront verification, and some market themselves as “low‑KYC” or “no‑KYC”. However, almost all licensed operators can request identity and address documents for regulatory, security, or risk reasons. You should carefully read each site’s KYC and AML policies and never assume that long‑term play or large withdrawals will remain anonymous.

How fast are crypto withdrawals?

Once an account and withdrawal have been approved, many crypto casinos aim to process payouts within minutes to under an hour, although additional time is needed for blockchain confirmations. Manual reviews, bonus checks, and higher‑risk transactions can significantly extend processing times, and no casino can guarantee instant payouts in every situation.
